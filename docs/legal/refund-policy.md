# SplitSaathi — Refund & Dispute Policy

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026  

---

## 1. Core Operating Principle
**SplitSaathi does not process, handle, intermediate, or escrow payments.**

The SplitSaathi application is strictly an expense calculator and ledger tracking tool. When you initiate a settlement by tapping "Pay via UPI", SplitSaathi dispatches an operating system intent to your device's banking/UPI app (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM).

## 2. In-App Fee Refunds
- SplitSaathi is free to use. No subscription fees, paywalls, or premium tier charges are debited by SplitSaathi.
- Consequently, there are no software fee refunds or return mechanisms administered by SplitSaathi.

## 3. Peer-to-Peer Payment Disputes & Erroneous Transfers
Because all financial transactions occur outside of SplitSaathi:
1. **Wrong Recipient or Wrong Amount:** If you transfer funds to an incorrect UPI ID or enter the wrong monetary amount in your external UPI app, SplitSaathi cannot reverse, cancel, or refund the transaction.
2. **Resolution Mechanism:**
   - **Step 1 (Informal Resolution):** Contact the recipient directly within your group or flat to request a reverse UPI transfer.
   - **Step 2 (Banking Channels):** Contact your remitting bank or raise a transaction dispute through the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) UPI portal or your specific UPI service provider using your 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) number.
3. **In-App Dispute Marker:**
   - If a peer records an incorrect or fraudulent "I Paid" claim inside a SplitSaathi group, the expense creator can tap **"Dispute"** and enter a documented reason.
   - Disputing marks the payment record as disputed and prevents the contribution from closing until the peers resolve the discrepancy.
